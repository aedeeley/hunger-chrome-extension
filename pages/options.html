GOOD TO KNOW

Helpful boundaries, honestly.

Hunger matches a curated list of domains, names, and ordering phrases. It can’t recognize every service, unlabeled image, video ad, or native app. Some matching articles will be hidden, too. Groceries on general shopping sites aren’t covered automatically.

Everything is processed locally. No accounts, analytics, browsing history logs, or remote requests. Chrome’s “read and change” permission lets Hunger hide content on the pages you visit. After installing or reloading the extension, refresh existing tabs. Chrome’s own pages and the Chrome Web Store can’t be filtered.

Switching filtering off restores hidden content. Blocked network resources may need a page refresh. Incognito works only if you allow it in Chrome’s extension settings.
